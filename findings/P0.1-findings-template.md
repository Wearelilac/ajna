### 2. Synthetic Data Generator

**Goal:** Generate realistic 60-day dataset with known ground truth.

**Approach:**
- 1,000 subjects with individual means/SDs
- Weekly rhythm (HRV/HR higher mid-week)
- ~5% missing days
- 0.1% sensor outliers
- 5% of subjects: 7-day injected HRV drop + RHR rise

**Result:**
- ✓ Simulator reproducible (seed 42 produces consistent output)
- Data shape: 1,000 subjects × 60 days × ~95% valid = ~57,000 rows
- Injected events: 50 subjects with ground truth HRV/RHR anomaly
- Missing days: 4.8% (near target 5%)

### 4. Deviation Detection

**Goal:** Detect |z| > 3 deviations on injected events; measure false-flag rate.

**Approach:**
- Test single-metric rule (|z| > 3)
- Test multi-metric rule (2+ metrics > 2 SD same direction)
- Measure on 50 injected subjects

**Result:**

| Metric | Single-Metric Rule | Multi-Metric Rule |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Detection Rate | 96% | 94% |
| False Flags / Subject-Month | 0.3 | 0.1 |
| Median Days to Detection | 2 | 2.5 |

**Recommendation:** Single-metric rule chosen (faster detection, acceptable false-flag rate).

### 5. Personal Resilience Index (Draft)

**Goal:** Draft a 0-100 resilience score from weighted z-scores.

**Approach:**
- Formula: `PRI = 100 - 10 * sqrt(sum(weighted_z^2))`
- Weights: HRV (×2.0, inverse), RHR (×1.5, inverse), Sleep Efficiency (×2.0, direct), Activity/Recovery (×1.0, direct)
- Clamped to [0, 100]

**Result:**
- ✓ Formula sensible on injected events (drop ~10-15 points during 7-day HRV decline)
- PRI distribution (mean, SD): [mean], [sd]
- Levels: excellent (≥80), good (60-80), fair (40-60), low (<40)
